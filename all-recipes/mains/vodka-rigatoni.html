Vodka Rigatoni

Ingredients

  • Rigatoni
  • Chicken
  • Guanciale or bacon
  • Broccoli
  • Onion
  • Shallots
  • Olive Oil
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Heavy Cream
  • Vodka

Instructions

  1. Chop guanciale or bacon into small chunks
  2. Cook guanciale or bacon in pan to start building the flavor
  3. Cut chicken into small pieces
  4. Cook chicken in same pan as guanciale or bacon
  5. Optional: Cut chicken skin into small pieces and sautee with shallots
  6. Sautee onions in the oils from the guanciale, bacon, and chicken
  7. Steam broccoli
  8. Pour vodka into pan used to cook meats and let it cook off on medium for about 10-15 minutes
  9. Once vodka has reduced, add tomato sauce to the pan
  10. Add heavy cream to the sauce
  11. Season sauce to your liking
  12. Add meats and broccoli to sauce
  13. Let sauce simmer for 20-30 minutes to build depth
  14. Boil noodles
  15. Add noodles to sauce and enjoy
