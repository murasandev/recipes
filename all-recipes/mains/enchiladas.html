Enchiladas

Ingredients

  • Corn Tortillas
  • Chicken or Beef
  • Cheese
  • Salsa Verde
  • Cilantro
  • Onions

Instructions

  1. Cook chicken or beef
  2. Shred chicken or prepare beef into smaller chunks
  3. Fry tortillas in oil on low to medium heat, about 5-10 secs per side
  4. Wrap chicken or beef and cheese into tortillas
  5. Pour salsa over enchiladas
  6. Add cheese and onions on top
  7. Bake at 350 degrees for 25 minutes
  8. Top with cilantro and cream
