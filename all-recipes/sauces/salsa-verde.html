Salsa Verde

Ingredients

  • Tomatillos
  • White Onion
  • Cilantro
  • Lime
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel tomatillos and onions
  2. Boil tomatillos and onions together with some salt
  3. Blend tomatillos, onions and cilantro
  4. Cook salsa with oil on medium heat, adds a roasted flavor
  5. Season with salt, lime, cilantro, and chopped onion
